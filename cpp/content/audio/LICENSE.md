## Sound effects

`collect.wav`, `win.wav`, `loss.wav`, and `restart.wav` are original, generated
sound effects created for this repository from mathematical oscillators. They
contain no third-party recordings and are distributed under the repository's
MIT license. All four use 48 kHz, signed 16-bit mono PCM WAV encoding for
portable XNA `SoundEffect::FromStream` loading.

The previously evaluated NOX SOUND samples are not present in these files or in
the shipped content.
